# Spur

**What it is** — The terminal to the stem of a rounded letter (the typography-fundamentals skill's exact definition) — the stroke that exits the base of the stem, curls right, and lifts. Clarendon finishes its a here rather than planting a serif on the baseline.

**Status** — Live. Cited: outer contour walk, points 750–844, running (357,5) to (492,−2) to (545,64) to (492,174).

**Hits** — The rhythm of the word. The spur sets the optical gap to whatever letter follows, so it is doing spacing work, not decoration. It is why a needs different kerning than o does.

**Does not hit** — The foot serif, because there isn't one. This is the tripwire this whole map exists to mark: see the ghost card at [foot-serif](foot-serif.md). It also isn't a "tail" — the skill reserves that term for Q, K, R, and the loop of g; see [naming-collisions](../reference/naming-collisions.md).
