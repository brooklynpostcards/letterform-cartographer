# Counter

**What it is** — The enclosed white shape inside the bowl. In Clarendon it is small and tensioned, which is a large part of why the face reads dense at text sizes.

**Status** — Live, but derived. Cited: the glyph's second contour, bounds x 157–323, y 60–247, centroid about (215,120).

**Hits** — Nothing directly, and that is the finding. The counter is the one noun on this map with no geometry you can edit. To change it you change the [bowl](bowl.md) or the [stem](stem.md) and the counter follows.

**Does not hit** — The aperture. Everyone reaches for these two as one word. The counter is the enclosed space; the aperture is the way out of it. Different shapes, different borders. See [aperture](aperture.md).
