# Bowl

**What it is** — The lower curved stroke that sweeps left, dips under the baseline, and returns to meet the spur. It is the stroke that closes the counter.

**Status** — Live. Cited: outer contour walk, points 525–700, running (311,396) to (24,130) to (203,−12) to (276,12).

**Hits** — The counter directly: the counter has no geometry of its own, it is whatever the bowl and the stem leave behind. Also the bottom overshoot, since the bowl is the stroke that dips to −15.

**Does not hit** — The finial. In a double-storey a these read as one continuous gesture, but they are separate strokes with separate jobs: the bowl closes a shape, the finial ends in open air at a terminal. See [finial](finial.md).
