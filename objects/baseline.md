# Baseline

**What it is** — y = 0. The line the type sits on, and the origin every other measurement on this map is quoted from.

**Status** — Live. Cited: the font's coordinate origin; every y value in this map is relative to it.

**Hits** — Nothing inside this glyph. It is the reference, not a part. Moving it moves the entire font's relationship to its own metrics, not the a.

**Does not hit** — The bottom of the letter. The a reaches −15, below the baseline, on purpose. See [overshoot](overshoot.md).
