# Foot serif — GHOST

**What it is** — Nothing. There is no foot serif on this glyph.

**Status** — Ghost. A name with no wiring. Cited: the outer contour runs continuously from the stem's base at (444,74) into the spur at (545,64) with no bracketed slab anywhere along it. Compare n, m, or i in the same font, which all plant one.

**Why it has a card at all** — Clarendon is a slab serif. That is the first and often the only thing anyone knows about it, and it produces a confident, wrong expectation that every stroke in the face ends in a bracketed slab. The lowercase a does not. Its stem terminates in a curved [spur](spur.md) and its finial terminates in a [ball terminal](ball-terminal.md).

This was verified the hard way while building this map. The extracted outline was assumed corrupt precisely because it lacked the expected foot serif, and was only accepted after cross-checking a second, independent Clarendon (SuperClarendon) which agreed exactly. The wrong expectation cost real time. That is what a ghost card is for.

**Hits** — Nothing. There is nothing here to change.

**Does not hit** — Everything. Any instruction that says “adjust the foot serif on the a” is describing a different typeface, or a different letter in this one.
