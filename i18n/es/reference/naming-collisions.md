## Nombrar colisiones en este territorio

Escritas una vez para que nadie las vuelva a descubrir adivinándolas de una carta más familiar.

### "Arch" no es la palabra de esta carta — "Final" es

Por analogía con *n*, *m* y *h*, la palabra natural para el trazo superior curvo de esta *a* es “arco”. El glosario de habilidades de tipografía-fundamentos no define “arco” en absoluto, y por una buena razón: un arco regresa a la línea base en un pie (como en *n*/*m*/*h*); este trazo termina al aire libre en un terminal. El término real de la habilidad, definido específicamente para *a* y *f*, es **finial**. Véase [`objects/finial.md`](../objects/finial.md).

### "Tail" tampoco es la palabra de esta letra — "Spur" es

The skill defines “tail” only for *Q*, *K*, *R*, and the loop of *g* — all strokes that exit *downward* past the baseline. This *a*'s bottom-right stroke stays on the baseline and curls up, which the skill separately defines as a **spur**: “the terminal to the stem of a rounded letter.” See [`objects/spur.md`](../objects/spur.md). Both this and the finial/arch collision above were caught only by auditing the drafted cards against the skill's own glossary after writing them from memory first — see [`decisions.md`](decisions.md).

### “Contador” y “Apertura” no son la misma forma blanca

Both sit in the same neighborhood — inside and around the bowl — which makes them read as one gap from a distance. They have different borders: the counter is the fully enclosed shape inside the bowl (its own contour); the aperture is the opening in the outer silhouette between the finial's ball terminal and the bowl. Editing one does not move the other. See [`objects/counter.md`](../objects/counter.md) and [`objects/aperture.md`](../objects/aperture.md).

### "Foot serif" se lee como real porque Clarendon es una serif de losa

The single most likely wrong assumption in this territory: Clarendon's category name promises a slab serif at the foot of every stroke. This letter's stem ends in a spur, not a serif. See the ghost card, [`objects/foot-serif.md`](../objects/foot-serif.md).
